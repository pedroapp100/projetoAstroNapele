### Inferences
- The most patient-relevant real differentiators are likely: (1) a named, credentialed person who leads and appears in the consultation, (2) evaluation-first planning, (3) a calm, high-end space, (4) skin-of-color expertise IF documented. Of these only (2) and (3) are fully supported by material the clinic controls today. (1) and (4) need her credentials, case photos (with consent) and her own explanation.
- Removing or softening unverifiable superlatives ("a única", "referência") and replacing them with checkable specifics (professional name + council number, years, certifications, number of cases, Google rating and count) is both safer and more persuasive.
- "Pele negra" positioning would be a strong, scarce proposition in a mid-size city only if she can show training, before/after of dark skin (with consent) and patient voices; right now it is a claim without evidence in public sources.
- The (71) number could be turned into a story ("baiana em Anápolis") only if she confirms and wants it; otherwise leave it out.
